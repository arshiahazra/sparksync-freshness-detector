# sparksync-freshness-detector
o///o silly clg project: Research project exploring gas-sensor-based freshness detection and ML classification of fruits and vegetables.

## Overview

Fruits and vegetables can undergo internal ripening and spoilage before visible changes become obvious. This makes freshness assessment difficult through visual inspection alone and can contribute to food waste, economic loss, and potential health risks.

**SparkSync** explores the use of low-cost gas sensors to detect changes in volatile gases released by fruits and vegetables during different stages of ripening and spoilage.

The current research focuses on gas-sensor-based freshness detection and machine-learning classification.
